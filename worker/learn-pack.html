Form 2 English Revision Pack

Three original practice sets with worked answers · 30 marks

Classroom practice material. Teachers may print copies for their learners.

1. Reading comprehension · 10 marks

On Friday, the footbridge was closed for repairs. Musa took the longer road to school and noticed water running from a pipe beside the road. He nearly walked past: he was already late. Instead, he wrote down the nearest house number. At school he asked the caretaker who to call. By lunchtime, the caretaker had passed the information to the water office. On Monday the pipe was dry and a bright new fitting caught the morning light.

  1. Why did Musa take the longer road? (1)
  2. What did he notice? (1)
  3. Why did he nearly walk past? (1)
  4. What does writing down the house number suggest about Musa? Give evidence. (2)
  5. What probably happened over the weekend? Cite two clues. (3)
  6. Suggest a title that captures the main idea and explain it. (2)

2. Grammar and editing · 10 marks

The school garden need water every morning. Last Tuesday, two learners brings buckets before class. They filled the buckets at the tap, and carried it carefully to the beds. When the bell rang the group put away their tools.

  1. Correct “need” and “brings”. Explain one of your corrections. (3)
  2. Which pronoun should replace “it”? Why? (2)
  3. Rewrite the final sentence with punctuation that marks the opening clause. (1)
  4. Join “The beds were dry” and “The class watered them” using so. (2)
  5. Replace “put away” with a verb that keeps the meaning, and use it in the corrected final sentence. (2)

3. Practical writing · 10 marks

Write an 80–120 word email to your school’s gardening club. Propose a weekly watering rota, explain why it is needed, and ask members to choose a day. Include a subject line, greeting and sign-off.

Marking: clear purpose and reasons (3), workable rota and request for a reply (3), email structure and suitable tone (2), accurate language (2).

Worked answers

1. Reading

  1. The footbridge was closed for repairs.
  2. Water was running from a pipe.
  3. He was already late for school.
  4. He was resourceful or responsible; he noted the nearest house number so the leak could be located.
  5. The pipe was likely repaired. It was dry on Monday and had a new fitting.
  6. “A Small Action, a Fixed Leak” is suitable: reporting what he saw led to a likely repair. Accept equivalent justified titles.

2. Grammar

  1. Needs and brought. “Garden” is singular, so it takes “needs”; “Last Tuesday” sets the action in the past, so use “brought.”
  2. Them, because it refers to the plural “buckets.”
  3. “When the bell rang, the group put away their tools.”
  4. “The beds were dry, so the class watered them.”
  5. “When the bell rang, the group stored their tools.” Accept an equivalent verb in the correct tense.

3. Model email

Subject: Choose a day for the garden watering rota

Hello Gardening Club,

Some of our seedlings wilted last week because nobody was sure who should water them. I suggest that two members water the garden before class each weekday. On Fridays, the pair can also check the beds and record which plants need extra attention. We can put the rota by the classroom door so everyone can see it. If you cannot make your day, please arrange a swap in advance.

Please reply by Thursday with the day you can help and the name of a partner if you have one.

Thank you,
Thandi
