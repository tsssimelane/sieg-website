      01 / LINES

      Line Smoothing

      GeoJSON / SHP → smoother lines

      Round sharp corners in GPS tracks and other linework. Open line endpoints stay fixed; smoothing can shift the line between them.

      Use GeoJSON or a ZIP containing one line shapefile with its matching .prj file. Shapefile results download as WGS 84 GeoJSON.

      02 / CADASTRE

      Cadastre L-P

      Boundary lines → polygons

      Convert boundary lines into polygons. The tool splits crossings, removes duplicate edges, and can snap small gaps. Open edges are flagged for review.

      Use WGS 84 GeoJSON or a single line shapefile ZIP with its .prj file. Up to 7,000 line segments.

      Start at 0 to leave gaps open. Enter a small tolerance only if nearby ends belong together.

      Downloads are GeoJSON. Review the results before using them as parcel boundaries. Files are processed on your device.

      03 / RASTER

      Georeferencer

      Map image + reference → located raster

      Match a scanned map to a projected shapefile. Add at least three control points spread across the image.

      Include one shapefile and its matching .prj file.

      Select a point on the image, then click the matching location on the shapefile map.

      1. Image
      2. Shapefile

        The ZIP contains a PNG copy, its world file, the WGS 84 projection, and the control points. Processing stays on your device.

        04 / DISTANCE

        Distance Nodes

        Line geometry → evenly spaced points

        Place points at regular distances along each line part. Distances follow the line from its start.

        Use WGS 84 GeoJSON or a ZIP containing one line shapefile and its .prj file.

        05 / COORDINATES

        Coordinates Converter

        Excel coordinates → converted workbook

        Upload an Excel workbook, choose its coordinate columns, and append converted X and Y columns to a new copy.

        Use headers in row 1. Supported: WGS 84 (4326), Web Mercator (3857), and WGS 84 UTM (32601–32660 north, 32701–32760 south).

        06 / RASTER / VECTOR

        Raster Attribute Join

        Raster + vector → enriched features

        Add a raster value to each vector feature without changing its geometry or existing fields.

        Use WGS 84 GeoJSON or a shapefile ZIP with its .prj. The raster must use WGS 84, Web Mercator, or WGS 84 UTM.

        Points use the cell they fall in. Lines use their midpoint on the raster grid. Polygons use cells whose centres are inside. NoData is skipped.

          FIELD NOTES / PRACTICAL KNOWLEDGE

          SIEG Blog

          Useful guidance for better fieldwork and clearer maps.

          Read · Learn · Apply
          01FIELDWORK / GPSHow to get a better GPS fix in the fieldA few simple habits for more dependable phone measurements.

          Start outdoors with a clear view of the sky and give your phone time to settle. If the reported accuracy stays above 15 m, step away from buildings or dense trees and wait for a new fix. Record polygon corners where the boundary changes direction. Phone GPS is useful for field notes and rough measurements; use surveyed coordinates for legal boundaries.

          02GIS / DATAWhat to do with your shapefile ZIPOpen your layers, keep the files together and choose the right CRS.

          Unzip the download and keep each layer’s .shp, .shx, .dbf and .prj together. Open the .shp in QGIS or ArcGIS. The export uses WGS 84 (EPSG:4326) and includes a separate GPS points layer with UTC timestamps, reported accuracy and the name of the parent feature. Use a suitable local projected CRS if you need to measure the exported geometry in metres.
