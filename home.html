Who we are

Beyond what you see.
Towards what is possible.

SIEG brings together geospatial intelligence, farm management, environmental monitoring, property exploration, digital addressing, transport, learning, local expertise and an international business directory. Explore your world at every scale — from a global overview to your own field, catchment, property or community. SIEG brings together twelve connected services: SIEG Fields, SIEG Irrigate, SIEG Ledger, SIEG Chemicals, SIEG Hydrology, SIEG Eco, SIEG Estate, SIEG Address, SIEG Transit, SIEG Learn, SIEG Experts and SIEG Business.

Our vision is to make complex information easier to understand, so that people can make informed choices about places, resources and opportunities.
