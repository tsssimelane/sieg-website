About arrival times & privacy

Arrival times use distances between stops and phone speed, with a 30 km/h fallback. Traffic is not included. Waiting signals expire after 30 minutes. This browser gets a random device ID, stored locally and linked to your signed-in SIEG account. Clearing browser data creates a new ID. It is not your phone’s hardware ID.

Phone tracking is off. No separate tracking device required.
