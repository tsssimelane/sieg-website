FLOOD WATCH / INPUTS

How to use Flood Watch

Choose and analyse a basin or polygon. For river buffers, upload one WGS84 river centreline, enter channel width, and provide surveyed water-level thresholds for connected locations 40 m, 70 m and 100 m from the bank. Use timestamped gauge readings or an explicit rise-rate scenario. For dams, enter current storage and the storage threshold at a known spillway or alert level, then supply inflow and outflow. All times are UTC and relative to the latest reading or entered reference. Distance alone cannot establish flood arrival.

Date: YYYY-MM-DD. Time: HH:MM[:SS] in UTC. Leave unavailable water-level or flow values blank. Use matching stations and datums. In Excel, import as comma-delimited CSV.

River buffers

Buffers measure proximity from the assumed bank, not predicted inundation. A threshold applies to its surveyed location, not the entire zone. Manual updates; no live sensor feed.

Dam threshold

Constant net-inflow scenario. Spillway onset is not dam failure or downstream flood arrival. Use reservoir routing for changing inflows or outflows.
